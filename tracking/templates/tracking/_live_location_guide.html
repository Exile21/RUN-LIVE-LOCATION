How to share Live Location

  1. Open the bot link on your phone and press Start.
  2. Press Agree and share in the private chat.
  3. Tap the attachment icon, choose Location, then Share Live Location.

Share in the private bot chat, not the group. A one-time location pin is not tracked. Keep sharing until the event ends. Each runner can review their own track after the event; it is deleted 30 days after close. Use /stop to leave and delete it.
